Publications

FA-RDP: A Frequency-Adaptive Reactive Diffusion Policy for Contact-Rich Manipulation

Lifeng Zhuo*, Wendi Chen*, Han Xue, Shirun Tang, Jun Lv, Cewu Lu, Chuan Wen

RA-L 2026 · Under review · * Equal contribution

PDF arXiv Project

A visual-force diffusion policy that adapts its control frequency to preserve diverse motions before contact and react quickly to force feedback during contact-rich manipulation.

IPR-1: Interactive Physical Reasoner

CVPR 2026 · * Equal contribution

PDF arXiv Project Code

NeurIPS 2026 · Accepted

PDF arXiv
